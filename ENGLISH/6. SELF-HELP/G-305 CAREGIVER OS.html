Local Data & Privacy Architecture

CAREGIVER OS™ is built with a local-first architecture. This application is designed to keep your entries in your browser's local storage and does not require an account or server database.

Security Guidelines

  • Local Storage Only: Your care plans, medication lists, and reflections stay inside this browser. No external API calls are made.
  • Device Security: Use CAREGIVER OS™ on a personal or password-protected device to protect sensitive care records.
  • Data Control: You can wipe all history entries instantly using the clear options in the Unified History tab.
For practical planning and organization only. Not medical or legal advice. For health decisions, consult a physician. For legal matters, consult an attorney. Burnout Check results are self-reflection indicators, not clinical diagnoses.
