Data Control & Security

Privacy & Local Data Protection

MY CAREER, MY LIFE™ is designed as a browser-native, client-side toolkit. Your career reflections, financial figures, and family conversation inputs are processed entirely within your browser.

Core Privacy Commitments

  • Local Storage Only: All history records are saved in your browser's LocalStorage. No server, database, or backend account exists.
  • No External Transmission: User inputs are never sent to external AI services or tracking systems.
  • Complete User Control: You can clear your saved records at any time using the Saved Analyses tab.
