Privacy & Local Storage Notice

100% Client-Side Architecture: LifeDirection™ operates entirely inside your web browser. No accounts, login credentials, or external servers are used.

Local Browser Storage: Your inputs, clarity index scores, and experiment logs are saved locally on your device via localStorage (namespace: lifedirection_v2).

Data Control: You can clear your saved history at any time using the "Clear History" options in individual tools or from the central History view.

Planning Aid Disclaimer: Match scores, risk estimates, and metrics generated by this app are heuristic decision frameworks designed to prompt reflection and lower experimentation barriers. They do not constitute formal career counseling or financial advisory guarantees.
