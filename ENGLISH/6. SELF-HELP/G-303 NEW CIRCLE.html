7-Day New Circle Reset

A structured, low-pressure weekly sequence designed to walk you through the entire system step-by-step.

DAY 1: See Your Baseline

Complete the Connection Audit to understand your structural starting point without self-blame.

DAY 2: Find Your Low-Pressure Entry Point

Use Low-Stakes Entry to identify one third space or focus-activity group nearby.

DAY 3: Turn Solo into Social

Build an Interest Bridge around something you already do alone (reading, coffee, working out).

DAY 4: Show Up Again

Return to the chosen spot. Focus purely on familiar faces—not instant deep connection.

DAY 5: Send One Low-Pressure Follow-Up

Use the Follow-Up Kit to message an acquaintance or extend a low-stakes invitation.

DAY 6: Check Your Social Battery

Run a Social Stamina reflection to adjust your pacing and maintain recovery routines.

DAY 7: Review & Synthesize Plan

Review your master plan and lock in one recurring social anchor for the month ahead.

Privacy & Safety Architecture

NEW CIRCLE™ operates on a strict local-first, privacy-conscious foundation.

1. Local Storage Security

All data entered into NEW CIRCLE™ is stored directly inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). No accounts are required, no external databases are utilized, and no personal entries are transmitted to any server.

2. Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

NEW CIRCLE™ is a behavioral workflow and practical social integration engine. It is NOT a clinical tool, diagnostic instrument, or therapy replacement. Scores, statuses, and output messages are reflection heuristics designed to help you organize your social exposure safely.

3. Relational Safety & Boundaries

The system actively encourages healthy reciprocity, emotional boundaries, and public-space safety. It explicitly discourages excessive messaging, chasing unreciprocated contact, or disclosing sensitive personal identification data to strangers.
