Life Transition Kit™

Navigate major life shifts smoothly—re-build your identity, routines, and expectations with clear, structured planning tools.

Planning Principle

The goal is not to recreate your previous life exactly, but to build a workable version of your new stage that preserves core values while letting go of outdated expectations. Start with the area causing the most friction.

1. Identity Architect

Reframe your self-concept and bridge past values into your new role.

2. Routine Re-Designer

Re-anchor daily schedules around your current energy constraints.

3. Role & Expense Auditor

Map new financial impacts and align responsibility distribution.

4. Boundary Resetter

Set clear expectations and reduce capacity guilt with customized scripts.

5. Milestone Log

Track adaptation progress, recognize wins, and reflect on growth over time.

Milestone Entries

30/90-Day Guided Transition Reset

A structured step-by-step roadmap to walk you through the system sequentially without overwhelm.

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1–7)

  • Day 1–2: Complete Identity Architect to map values to carry forward.
  • Day 3–4: Run Routine Re-Designer to adapt your schedule to current constraints.
  • Day 5: Audit costs and responsibilities with Role & Expense Auditor.
  • Day 6: Set key capacity limits with Boundary Resetter.
  • Day 7: Review your unified Life Transition Plan.

Phase 2: Stabilization (Days 8–30)

Execute one small adjustment per week. Log weekly wins in the Milestone Log to build visible adaptation momentum.

Phase 3: Integration (Days 31–90)

Re-run the assessment sliders on the Dashboard monthly to track changes in groundedness and adjust routines as your transition evolves.
