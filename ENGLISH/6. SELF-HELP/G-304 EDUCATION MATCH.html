Privacy & Data Architecture

100% Client-Side Private Storage

Local Storage Only

EducationMatch™ operates entirely within your web browser. All inputs, calculations, scripts, and trial agreements are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. No data is ever transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party trackers.

Complete Data Control

You can clear your local history at any time within each tool's history drawer or by clearing your browser cache. Exporting your plan is performed directly via your local clipboard.
