            7-Day Academic Confidence Reset

            A structured, step-by-step pathway using the core tools to rebuild academic self-efficacy in one week.

            Day 1: De-Escalate Peer Comparison

            Run Tool 1 to separate observable peer actions from unseen factors and establish your starting baseline.

            Day 2: Isolate Your Specific Skill Bottleneck

            Run Tool 2 to pinpoint the exact sub-skill causing friction instead of broad subject anxiety.

            Day 3: Build a 20-Minute Practice Loop

            Run Tool 5 to convert your skill bottleneck into an active retrieval routine.

            Day 4: Audit Your Growth Evidence

            Run Tool 3 to log baseline vs. current evidence and identify invisible wins over the last 30 days.

            Day 5: Rehearse Your Pre-Test Protocol

            Run Tool 4 to draft your physical reset, cognitive reframe, and first-5-minutes exam strategy.

            Day 6: Execute One Focused Practice Session

            Run one 20-minute study sprint using your Day 3 plan and log the result.

            Day 7: Consolidate Your Academic Plan

            Review your Academic Confidence Plan, set one primary priority, and set your 30-day review target.
