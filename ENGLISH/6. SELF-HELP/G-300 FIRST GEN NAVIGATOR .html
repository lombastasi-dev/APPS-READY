Guided Sequence

First-Gen 7-Day Navigator

A structured, step-by-step sequence through university navigation without cognitive overload. Follow one day at a time.

Day 1: Decode the University

Use the Academic Decoder to understand office hours or syllabi protocols. Unlearn the myth that asking questions means you don't belong.

Day 2: Strengthen Academic Confidence

Use the Imposter Reset to reframe self-doubt during class discussions or grading feedback into recognized strategic strengths.

Day 3: Communicate with Family

Use the Family Translator to bridge family love and study time commitments with a compassionate boundary script.

Day 4: Find Your Campus Support System

Use the Resource Finder to identify emergency micro-grants, tutoring centers, or student success offices.

Day 5: Build Your Career Bridge

Use Major-to-Career to translate your major and past work experience into professional skill terminology and alumni networking.

Day 6: Review Milestones & Snapshot

Check off completed foundational actions on the Dashboard milestone tracker and review your synthesized plan.

Day 7: Create Your 30-Day Action Plan

Compile your insights into My First-Gen Plan, print your checklist, and establish your 30-day review point.

Privacy & Security

About FirstGen Navigator™

FirstGen Navigator™ is built as a local-first student navigation operating system. It translates institutional opacity into clear, actionable steps for first-generation university students.

Local Storage & Privacy Guarantee

All history items, milestone checkboxes, and preferences are stored exclusively within your browser's localStorage. No personal data, questionnaire inputs, or generated scripts are ever transmitted to any external server or third party.

Educational Guidance Disclaimer

For educational guidance and personal development only. Not formal academic advisement, psychological therapy, legal counsel, or financial aid adjudication. Always verify institution-specific policies with your university registrar, academic advisor, or student success office.
