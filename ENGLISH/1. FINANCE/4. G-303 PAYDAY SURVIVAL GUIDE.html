Triage Operating Rules

  • First protect essential survival needs: food, shelter, essential transport, medication, and core utilities.
  • Do not treat every bill as equally urgent when available cash is insufficient.
  • Contact providers proactively before due dates pass whenever possible.
  • Use outputs as structured planning prompts; verify local regulations and provider terms directly.

Payday Survival Reset Sequence

Follow this 7-step guided workflow to systematically navigate the gap before your next payday without panic.

Day 1 — Count the Gap & Freeze Drains

Set your countdown target date and complete the Cash Leak Triage to determine your absolute daily spending limit.

Day 2 — Rank Priorities & Allocate Funds

Use Payment Priority to categorize housing, utilities, and obligations so essential survival needs are satisfied first.

Day 3 — Proactive Provider Outreach

For any obligations marked as held or delayed, use Panic-Free Negotiator to request formal payment extensions.

Day 4 — Protect Social Boundaries

Handle upcoming social obligations without debt using Embarrassment Shield copyable responses.

Day 5 — Explore Safe Income Opportunities

If essential cash gaps remain, use Micro-Payday Bridge to evaluate realistic micro-income options.

Day 6–7 — Review Plan & Maintain Hard Stop

Review your complete synthesized Survival Plan and commit to your daily cash limit until payday arrives.
