Privacy & Local Storage

Your data stays entirely within your local browser.

Strict Privacy Guarantee

DebtEscape™ is a completely self-contained client-side application. No debt figures, account balances, income numbers, or negotiation script entries are ever transmitted to an external server, API, analytics provider, or third party.

  • Local Browser Storage: All saved runs are kept in your browser's localStorage.
  • Shared Device Safety: If you are using a public or shared computer, clear your history using the button below or in the "My History" tab before closing your browser.
  • No Accounts / No Tracking: No sign-up, password, or account registration is required.

Educational & Planning Notice: DebtEscape™ provides structural financial calculations, heuristic strategy options, and call preparation templates for planning purposes only. It does not provide formal legal, tax, credit-repair, or certified professional financial advice. For complex hardship legalities or binding settlement terms, consult a qualified professional or accredited non-profit credit counseling agency (such as NFCC.org).
