Local Storage Security

All tool calculations, progress tracking, and outreach drafts remain stored exclusively inside your browser's local cache. No inputs or financial data are ever transmitted to an external server or remote database.

Always verify local permits, platform terms, and safety requirements before offering service. Do not enter unfamiliar private spaces without clear customer contact details, and establish agreed scope before commencing physical work.
