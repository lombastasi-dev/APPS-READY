Safety & Privacy Standards

Local Browser Storage Guarantee

Job Loss Reset™ is built as a self-contained web application. All financial numbers, personal dates, text scripts, and saved analysis histories remain strictly inside your device's browser local storage (localStorage). No data is transmitted to external servers.
