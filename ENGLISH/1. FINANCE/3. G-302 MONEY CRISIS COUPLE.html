Stabilization Principle

In a financial shock, couples need two forms of stabilization at the same time: Financial Stabilization (protecting housing, food, and basic necessities) and Relational Coordination (stopping blame and operating as a team). This app helps you navigate both safely without panic or humiliation.

7-Day Money Crisis Reset

Follow this day-by-day structured sequence to move step-by-step from chaos to a fully stabilized household plan.

Day 1: Face the Numbers

Run Crisis Triage to define current liquid runway and urgent overdue obligations.

Day 2: Build the Survival Budget

Separate essential shelter/food costs from pauseable spending to find your baseline.

Day 3: Prepare Creditor Calls

Generate custom scripts to ask landlords, banks, or utilities for hardship options.

Day 4: Stop the Blame Spiral

Align on accountability without humiliation and set a structured daily check-in.

Day 5: Set Recovery Milestones

Build 30-60-90 day condition-based targets for income and savings buffers.

Day 6 & 7: Synthesize & Review

Consolidate all outputs into your printable Household Crisis Plan.

Privacy & Safety Notice

LOCAL STORAGE & DATA PRIVACY
• All data entered into Money Crisis Couple™ is stored entirely within your own web browser's local storage (localStorage).
• No financial calculations, names, debts, or notes are transmitted to any external server or database.
• You can clear your saved entries at any time using the "Clear History" options in each tool or the Master History view.
RELATIONSHIP SAFETY & FINANCIAL COERCION
• Joint financial planning requires safety and mutual trust. If you are experiencing financial abuse, coercive control, or fear for your physical safety, joint planning may not be appropriate or safe.
• If you need support, consider reaching out safely to local community advocacy groups or professional legal/counseling services.

This application is a structured decision-support and communication toolkit for household planning purposes only. It does not constitute legal, tax, regulated debt adjustment, financial advice, or relationship therapy. For specific legal advice, consult a licensed attorney.
