7-Day Emergency Money Reset

A structured, step-by-step 7-day protocol to stabilize your emergency, negotiate terms, protect essential assets, and execute a post-crisis recovery plan.

Day 1 — Triage Urgency & Cash Allocation

Determine whether the bill affects essential survival/work and calculate your true shortfall.

Day 2 — Compare Payment Paths & Tradeoffs

Evaluate 0% provider options, personal item sales, and credit tools against relational risk.

Day 3 — Request Itemization & Provider Hardship

Call or write your provider to request line-item audits, prompt-pay discounts, or hardship waivers.

Day 4 — Seek Dignified Assistance if Needed

Use transparent, bounded outreach scripts to ask family, friends, or local organizations for assistance.

Day 5 — Establish Post-Crisis Debt & Budget Plan

Model realistic debt payoff timelines and enforce temporary 90-day budget reductions.

Day 6 — Synthesize Combined Emergency Plan

Review your overall position across all completed modules in one cohesive view.

Day 7 — Establish 30-Day Review Date & Reset Mindset

Set your next check-in milestone, step away from daily financial anxiety, and allow recovery to proceed.

Privacy & Safety Commitments

Local Browser Storage: Emergency Money Navigator™ operates entirely within your client web browser. All entered numbers, notes, and generated scripts are saved strictly to your device's localStorage.

No Data Transmission: Zero financial, debt, billing, or personal details are transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers or analytics platforms.

Decision Support Only: This software provides illustrative decision-planning frameworks and generic script templates. It does not provide regulated financial advice, legal counsel, or official medical billing representation.

Data Removal: You can completely clear all saved records at any time from the "Saved History" tab or via browser settings.
