Privacy, Local Storage & Security

How your workload information is handled.

100% Local Browser Execution: Workload Fairness™ runs entirely in your web browser. No form data, text entries, or generated scripts are transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or analytics providers.

Local Storage Notice: Your saved analyses and system state are stored locally on your device via browser localStorage. Clearing browser cookies or cache may remove saved data unless printed or exported.

Data Security Best Practice: Avoid including sensitive personal identifying numbers, confidential customer records, or proprietary secrets in free-text fields. Keep descriptions focused on operational roles and deliverables.
