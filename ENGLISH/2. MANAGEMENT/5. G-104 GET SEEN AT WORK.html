Guidance & Principles

Start Here: Core Visibility Principles

Visibility is not about talking about yourself constantly. It is about making valuable work easier for the right people to discover, understand, remember, and associate with you.

Strategic Guidance

  • Evidence, not ego: Anchoring updates in documented outcomes eliminates needy self-promotion theater.
  • Focus on your immediate gap: Do not try to solve your entire career visibility system in one session. Pick the tool closest to your current bottleneck.
  • Keep evidence factual: Do not exaggerate metrics or outcome claims. Ground your career narratives in true contributions.
  • Visibility is not a substitute for competence: High visibility only amplifies solid performance.
  • Recognize organizational limits: Lack of recognition does not automatically mean personal failure. If consistent value and visibility yield no progress, evaluate the environment.
