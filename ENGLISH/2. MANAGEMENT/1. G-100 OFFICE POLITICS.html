Core Operating Principles

Workplace politics is the natural human system for distributing resources and making decisions. The goal is not to become manipulative, but to cultivate strategic awareness while protecting your integrity.

Clarity, Not Paranoia Treat political observations as working hypotheses, not absolute facts about motives.
Reciprocal Alliances Build trust-based relationships rooted in mutual value, support, and professional respect.
Authentic Visibility Ensure key stakeholders recognize your contributions through team wins and structured metrics.
Values Protection Establish clear professional boundaries to safeguard your ethical standards under pressure.

Privacy & Data Security

100% Client-Side Privacy: Office Politics Navigator™ operates entirely inside your Web Browser. No personal names, notes, or political analyses are ever transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

Local Storage Details

Your inputs and saved strategy analyses are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage under versioned keys (officepolitics_v2_state). Clearing your browser data will erase this information.

Data Control Actions

For professional relationship strategy only. Not psychological, legal, or HR advice. For harassment, discrimination, or unlawful workplace conduct, contact appropriate organizational authorities or legal advisors.
