About First 90 Days™ System Scope & Safety

First 90 Days™ is a commercial career onboarding, organizational navigation, and first-quarter success system designed to assist newly hired or promoted professionals.

Local Privacy Guarantee: All inputs, analyses, and saved records remain strictly local to your web browser (`localStorage`). No information is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or company HR portals.

Important Scope & Boundary Disclaimers:

  • This product provides planning heuristics, conversational templates, and organizational frameworks. It is not HR, legal, or formal psychological advice.
  • Outputs are non-diagnostic working hypotheses meant to prompt productive dialogue and deliberate planning. They do not predict guaranteed employment outcomes or promotions.
  • Always align onboarding deliverables and commitments with your official company policies, manager expectations, and departmental guidelines.
For career onboarding planning only. Not a substitute for your company's official onboarding process. For HR or policy questions, consult your manager or HR representative. All data stored locally in browser.
