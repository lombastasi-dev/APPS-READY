Strategic Orientation & Workflow Guide

A salary conversation becomes substantially stronger when you separate what the market suggests, what you have actually contributed, when you ask, and what alternatives you hold.

  • Start with Market Value if you do not know your current compensation benchmark position.
  • Start with Contribution Case if you are under-recognized or taking on responsibilities beyond your title.
  • Use Negotiation Script Builder when you are ready to prepare your direct conversation strategy.
  • Use Timing Analyzer before putting a formal review meeting on your manager's calendar.
  • Use Plan B Calculator when you need external leverage, optionality, or an alternative growth path.

          100% Local Browser Security

          No accounts, no external tracking, no cloud servers.

          Your calculated salary ranges, contribution notes, negotiation scripts, timing audits, and exit options are processed entirely within your local browser runtime. Saved analyses are stored in this browser using local storage (`salaryreality_v2_` namespace).

          IMPORTANT STORAGE NOTICE

          Clearing your browser history/cookies, using private/incognito mode, or switching to a different device will reset local browser storage. For long-term retention, use the Print / Export PDF or Copy Summary buttons to save your career plans externally.
