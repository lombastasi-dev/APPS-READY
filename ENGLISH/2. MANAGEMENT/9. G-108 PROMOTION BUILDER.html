Privacy & Data Security

100% Client-Side Architecture — Your career data never leaves your browser.

Data Guarantee

PromotionBuilder is built completely using vanilla JavaScript and local browser storage (localStorage). No accounts, login details, or career inputs are ever transmitted to an external server or third-party tracking system.

Ethical Career Guidelines:

  • • Authorized Evidence Only: Only present accomplishments and project metrics you are explicitly authorized to share. Never export proprietary source code, confidential financial client lists, or internal IP.
  • • No Unethical Leverage: Build leverage through genuine scope, leadership, and verified business outcomes—never through manufactured threats or false external offers.
  • • Local Control: Clearing your browser storage or using private browsing mode will reset your saved history. You can clear your data at any time from the History menu.
