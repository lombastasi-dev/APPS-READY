Privacy & Safety

Private by design. Your reflections remain on your device. We do not send your data to any server.

Important: Breakup Decision is a self-guided reflection and planning tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention, legal advice, or a substitute for qualified professional care.

If the relationship involves abuse, threats, coercive control, stalking, serious addiction, or immediate danger, do not rely on these exercises. Prioritize safety and appropriate professional support.

Never confront an unsafe partner based on a generated exercise.

For entertainment and reflection purposes only. Not therapy or professional advice. If you are in an abusive relationship, contact a domestic violence hotline or emergency services.
