Start Here / Privacy & Safety
Important context before beginning your relationship reflection journey.
Private by Design
Your reflections are intended to remain on your device. Reconnect is a self-guided reflection tool, not a clinical service. All entries, calculations, and stored histories stay entirely inside your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to external servers.
Non-Clinical Reflection Tool
Important: Reconnect is a self-guided reflection and communication tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention, or a substitute for qualified professional care.
Safety Notice
If your relationship involves abuse, threats, coercive control, serious addiction, immediate danger, or another situation where direct confrontation could put someone at risk, do not rely on these exercises as your primary source of help. Prioritize safety and seek appropriate professional support or local helpline resources immediately.

7-Day Reconnect Reset
A structured 7-day guided journey moving step-by-step through discovery to action.

Complete each day's reflection module in sequence. As you complete them, your overarching personalized Reconnect Plan will be automatically generated.

Day 1 Where Are We?
Roommate Reset

Assess distance, faded rituals, and emotional frequency.

Day 2 What's Creating Resentment?
Mental Load Reset

Examine household labor, invisible tracking, and ownership split.

Day 3 What Have We Stopped Noticing?
Appreciation Reset

Uncover blind spots, quiet contributions, and express gratitude.

Day 4 Can We Feel Close Without Pressure?
Intimacy Reset

Address physical distance, remove performance pressure, and build safety.

Day 5 What Needs to Change?
Crossroads

Clarify what repair requires, set boundaries, and define non-negotiables.

Day 6 The Reconnection Conversation
Script Generator

Synthesize your previous reflections into one clear, non-defensive conversation script.

Day 7 OUR NEXT 30 DAYS
30-Day Plan

Establish your core ongoing connection rituals for the next month.
