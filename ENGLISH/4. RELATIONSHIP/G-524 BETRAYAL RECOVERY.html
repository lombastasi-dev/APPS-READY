When This Tool Is Not Enough

Self-guided worksheets are not a substitute for safety support.

If your situation includes any of these, prioritize appropriate support:

  • Immediate physical danger or threats
  • Violence, sexual violence, or coercive control
  • Stalking or credible safety concerns
  • Self-harm thoughts or inability to remain safe
  • Child-safety concerns
  • Severe inability to function
  • Legal or financial abuse requiring specialist advice

You do not need to solve a safety crisis with a self-guided worksheet. Seek appropriate professional or emergency support.

This application does not provide emergency services or fabricated hotline information. Use appropriate local emergency, safeguarding, medical, legal, or crisis resources available to you.

Privacy & Safety

Understand what this browser-based application stores.

Local browser storage

This version is designed to run without an account, backend, analytics, or remote database. Recovery state and saved reflections are stored in versioned browser localStorage keys. No relationship data is intentionally submitted to a server by the application logic.

Storage namespace: betrayalrecovery_v2_state, betrayalrecovery_v2_history, betrayalrecovery_v2_preferences

External fonts/icons may be requested by the browser because the interface retains its original external asset links.
