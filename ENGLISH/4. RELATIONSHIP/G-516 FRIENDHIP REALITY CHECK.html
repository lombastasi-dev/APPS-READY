Privacy & Safety

Private by design. Your reflections remain on your device. We do not send your data to any server.

Important: Friendship Reality Check is a self-guided reflection and planning tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention, legal advice, or a substitute for qualified professional care.

If a friendship involves threats, stalking, coercion, harassment, or retaliation risk, do not rely on these exercises. Prioritize safety and appropriate professional support.
