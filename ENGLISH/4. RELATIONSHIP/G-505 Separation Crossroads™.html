The Five Central Questions

  1. Can my current finances support the transition? (Two-Household Reset)
  2. What is happening emotionally—and what do I need right now? (Emotional Weather)
  3. What kind of contact with my spouse is helping rather than destabilizing me? (Contact Compass)
  4. How can we reduce the emotional burden on our children? (Kids in the Middle)
  5. What information do I need before making a major decision about the relationship? (The Decision Window)

      PATH A: EXPLORE REPAIR

      Requires mutual accountability, observable behavioral changes over time, and structured counseling.

      PATH B: CONTINUE SEPARATION

      Focuses on independent stabilization, financial untangling, and long-term peace over temporary comfort.

      PATH C: DECISION WINDOW

      Set a defined period (30–90 days) to observe patterns rather than promises before making an irreversible choice.

      LOOK FOR PATTERNS, NOT PROMISES

      Ask: Has actual behavior changed consistently over time? Are boundaries respected when tension arises? Are promises backed by verifiable action?

      100% Private & Local Storage Architecture

      Separation Crossroads™ operates entirely inside your web browser. No accounts are created, no personal data is transmitted to remote servers, and no external tracking databases are used. All history entries remain stored exclusively on your current device using local browser memory (localStorage).

      Safety Disclaimer & Limitations

      This toolkit is a self-guided reflection, stabilization, and planning system. It is NOT legal advice, financial advice, custody determination, psychotherapy, clinical diagnosis, or domestic violence intervention.

      If your relationship involves coercion, threats, stalking, physical abuse, or severe financial control, standard communication scripts and direct negotiations are unsafe. Please disengage from direct contact and reach out to appropriate local safeguarding services or legal representatives.
