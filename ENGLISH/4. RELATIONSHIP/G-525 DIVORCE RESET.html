Privacy / About

Understand how Divorce Reset handles your information.

Storage
Your saved results use browser localStorage.
Accounts
No account or backend is required.
Analytics
This application does not add analytics or remote divorce-data storage.
Scope
Planning and reflection only—not therapy, legal advice, financial advice, or a diagnostic assessment.
Because browser behavior, extensions, device backups, and browser synchronization can vary, this app does not make an absolute claim that information can never leave your device.

Safety

Use the app to organize next steps—not to escalate conflict.

If there are threats, stalking, violence, coercion, financial abuse, or an immediate safety concern:

Prioritize appropriate local emergency, legal, financial, or mental-health support. Do not use the app to confront, retaliate, monitor, access accounts without permission, or bypass a court order or formal agreement.

Important scope reminders

  • A cash-flow snapshot is not a complete financial plan.
  • Boundary guidance is general planning language; an actual order or agreement controls.
  • Dating categories are reflection prompts, not a scientifically validated readiness test.
  • You choose what to disclose; the app does not require sensitive details.
For reflection and planning purposes only. Not therapy, clinical assessment, legal advice, financial advice, or a guaranteed recovery program. If safety, legal, financial, or acute emotional issues exceed this app's scope, seek appropriate qualified local support.
