Start Here: Orientation

Time problems are often relationship design problems.

Two people can love each other deeply and still struggle with time. One person finishes work at 4:00 PM; the other finishes at 7:00 PM. One wants conversation immediately upon returning; the other needs silence to decompress. Neither means the relationship is failing — you may simply have different definitions of "enough."

Five Essential Questions We Will Solve Together:

  • 1. How much meaningful time do we actually have? (Tool 1: Time Audit)
  • 2. Can we ask for connection without creating defensiveness? (Tool 2: The Ask)
  • 3. How do we find windows when calendars clash? (Tool 3: Schedule Sync)
  • 4. Can we protect both togetherness and individual space? (Tool 4: Space + Togetherness)
  • 5. How do we know whether our new rhythm is actually working? (Tool 5: Weekly Check-In)
