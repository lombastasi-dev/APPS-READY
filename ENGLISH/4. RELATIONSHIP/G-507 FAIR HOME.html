Start Here / Privacy & Safety

Important context before beginning your household reflection journey. A quick guide to what Fair Home does, how your information is handled, and how to use the toolkit safely.

🔒 Private by Design

Fair Home is designed so your household reflections and saved histories stay in your browser's local storage. The current toolkit does not require an account or send your answers to a Fair Home database.

Note: Technical assets (fonts & CSS icons) may load from standard CDN libraries separately from your data.

❤️ Non-Clinical Reflection Tool

Fair Home is a self-guided household reflection and communication tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention, or a substitute for qualified professional medical care.

Scores & ratios are conversation aids, not psychological evaluations.

⚠️ Safety Notice

If your relationship involves abuse, threats, coercive control, serious addiction, or immediate danger where direct confrontation could put someone at risk, do not use Fair Home tools as a substitute for professional safety support.

Prioritize personal safety first.

Core Product Principle

Make the Invisible Visible

Resentment builds when one partner quietly assumes the mental and physical burden of managing a household. Fair Home provides concrete, non-judgmental frameworks to name tasks, negotiate transfers, set standards, and recalibrate weekly.

Tool 01

Chore Audit

Map 16 foundational tasks across Partner A and Partner B. Calculate task split gaps and identify invisible task concentration.

Tool 02

Invisible Work Map

Uncover cognitive burden across 6 domains: inventory, social planning, admin, family health, and emotional atmosphere.

Tool 03

The Handoff

Negotiate clean ownership transfers (Notice + Plan + Execute) and generate low-friction conversation scripts.

Tool 04

Standard Setter

Reconcile cleanliness mismatches. Agree on a Minimum Viable Home (MVH) baseline to eliminate perfectionism battles.

Tool 05

Weekly Pulse Review

Rate 5 key metrics weekly (Fairness, Appreciation, Follow-through, Energy, Stress). Detect system drift before it turns into resentment.
