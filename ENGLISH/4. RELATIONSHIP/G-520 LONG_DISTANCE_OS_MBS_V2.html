PRIVACY & SAFETY

Local-first by design.

The core app does not require an account or relationship-data backend. It stores your plan and history in this browser's local storage.

What is saved

  • Tool results you choose to submit.
  • Your Distance Plan.
  • Reunion date and Reset progress.
  • History records, capped at 30.
Do not use absolute privacy language: browser/device access and storage behavior depend on your environment.

Relationship safety

Long-distance jealousy is not evidence of betrayal. The app does not request passwords, recommend tracking, or suggest covert testing.

If there are threats, coercion, stalking, retaliation or immediate safety concerns: ordinary relationship scripts are not the priority. Consider a safer setting and appropriate local support.

Financial & travel safety

Visit Planner uses the budget you enter as a planning assumption. It does not provide investment, credit, tax or debt advice.

Future / immigration safety

Visa or immigration issues are treated as external dependencies. Check current official requirements and qualified professional guidance where needed; this app does not predict approval or legal timelines.
