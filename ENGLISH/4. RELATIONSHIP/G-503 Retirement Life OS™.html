Retirement is a Life Transition

Designing the life after work—not just funding it.

A paycheck can give your week structure. A workplace can give you people. A career can give you identity. And work can quietly organize your sense of purpose.

When work disappears, those things don't automatically replace themselves. That's why retirement planning needs to include more than money.

THE FIVE CORE QUESTIONS

  1. Money: Can my money support the life I want?
  2. Purpose: What gives my week meaning now?
  3. People: Who do I want around me?
  4. Energy: How will I protect my energy and independence?
  5. Contribution: What do I want to leave, build or give?
