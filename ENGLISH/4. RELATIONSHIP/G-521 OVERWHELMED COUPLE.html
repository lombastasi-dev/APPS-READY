Privacy & Safety

Local-first planning with clear boundaries.

What is stored

Structured inputs, outputs, experiments and review dates are stored in browser localStorage. No account or backend is required by the core application.

Safety first: If threats, coercion, violence or fear of retaliation are present, prioritize safety and appropriate real-world support over relationship optimization. Do not force confrontation because an app suggests it.
  • No relationship-quality score.
  • No diagnosis.
  • No individualized financial, employment or legal advice.
  • No surveillance or fidelity verdicts.
  • Outputs are structured reflection and planning prompts.
A calmer way to run real life together. For reflection and planning; not therapy, clinical diagnosis, or individualized financial/legal advice.
