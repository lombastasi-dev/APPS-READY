Orientation & Core Operating Principle

The Core Rule: Conflict is not the end of a relationship; unaddressed boundary violations and explosive reactions are. Focus your energy on your opening 60 seconds and calm de-escalation boundaries rather than scripting an entire multi-hour debate.

Safety First: If a situation involves domestic violence, physical threats, severe emotional abuse, or stalking, scripts alone are insufficient. Prioritize physical safety and professional intervention.

7-Day Conflict Courage Reset

Your step-by-step guided transformation workflow to build lasting interpersonal resilience.

Day 1

Assess Baseline & Reframe Threat

Calculate your readiness index on the dashboard and use Tool 2 to examine your catastrophic fears.

Day 2

Architect Your Opening Script

Draft your 4-part objective statement (Observation, Impact, Request, Outcome) and check for accusatory trigger words.

Day 3

Upgrade Language to Assertive

Run your draft through the Passive-to-Assertive Translator to eliminate weak apologies and qualifiers.

Day 4

Prepare De-escalation Deflections

Review tactical responses for defensive reactions, shutdowns, or raised voices so you aren't caught off guard.

Day 5

Somatic Grounding & Practice Delivery

Practice reading your script aloud twice in front of a mirror while maintaining a slow pace and downward vocal inflection.

Day 6

Conduct Conversation & Execute Time-Out

Deliver your opening 60 seconds. If emotions spike, use the 15-minute time-out script and return calm.

Day 7

Post-Talk Debrief & Physical Shake-Off

Release residual adrenaline with physical shaking, log your courage score, and celebrate your self-trust.
