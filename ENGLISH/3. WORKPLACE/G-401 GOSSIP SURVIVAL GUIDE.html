System Orientation & Operational Principles

Workplace rumors thrive on emotional reactivity, ambiguity, and backchannel amplification. This OS provides practical reflection and tactical action—not moral diagnosis.

1. Starve or Address Minor noise dies when starved; high-exposure rumors need surgical factual correction.
2. Zero Emotional Reaction Deliver facts dryly in under 15 seconds, then pivot back to deliverables.
3. Control the Inputs Implement strict info lockdowns on personal life when targeted.
4. Document Systematically Maintain private offline logs (MOMO Protocol) for hostility.

100% Client-Side Data Storage

This application operates entirely inside your web browser. No inputs, text descriptions, or audit results are ever transmitted to an external server, database, or cloud service. All history is preserved strictly in your browser's LocalStorage.
