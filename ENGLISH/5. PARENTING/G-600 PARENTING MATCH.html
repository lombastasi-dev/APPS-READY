Privacy & Safety

A visible explanation of what this browser version does.

Local-first: this version does not include a server, account system, analytics, or remote database. Your entries are stored using this browser's local storage. Browser extensions, synced browser profiles, shared devices, or other software may still affect where browser data is available.

Use the toolkit as a conversation aid

  • Outputs are deterministic reflections and practical prompts, not diagnoses.
  • The weekly index is not a scientific parenting score and should not be used to infer child wellbeing or parenting competence.
  • No parenting style is presented as universally superior.
  • The app does not tell a caregiver to stay, leave, punish, or force a child to take sides.
Safety override: if there is abuse, violence, coercive control, serious safety risk, or immediate danger, a public/private coordination rule should not be used to conceal or normalize the risk. Seek appropriate professional or emergency support.

Data controls

Start Over clears a module's current answers and result. Clearing history requires confirmation. “Clear All Saved Data” removes the ParentingMatch V2 state, history, and preferences created by this version.
