SAFETY & PRIVACY

Use the toolkit as a reflection and planning aid.

Immediate safety

If a teen expresses intent to self-harm, is in immediate danger, is missing, is experiencing abuse, or another urgent safety issue is present, seek appropriate emergency/professional help. Do not wait for a toolkit result.

Privacy

This version uses versioned browser localStorage for its core state and history. It does not require an account, backend, or analytics. Browser storage behavior can vary, so do not treat this as a guarantee that information can never leave your device.

What this app does not do

It does not diagnose depression, anxiety, substance use, trauma, attachment problems, or parenting quality. It does not determine what your teen is feeling and it does not recommend covert surveillance, humiliation, threats, or punitive control.
