Privacy & Safety

Browser-based storage

This application runs in your browser. When history is enabled, saved records are kept in browser storage maintained by the application. No account is required.

The application is designed without a server submission layer for family relationship data. Clearing history removes the records maintained by this application.

Safety boundary

This toolkit does not replace professional or emergency support for credible threats, exploitation, sexual exploitation, serious cyberbullying, self-harm concerns, dangerous adult contact, or immediate physical safety risks.

It does not recommend spyware, password theft, secret tracking, or treating ordinary privacy as proof of wrongdoing.
