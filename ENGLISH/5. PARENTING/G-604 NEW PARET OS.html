Privacy & Data Storage Policy

Your private relationship and family data never leaves your personal browser.

100% Local Storage

NEW PARENT OS™ operates entirely on client-side technology. Inputs, calculation history, and reflections are saved strictly in your browser's localStorage. There are zero remote servers, databases, or third-party analytics connected to your inputs.

Private Reflections Handling

Weekly reflections entered in Module 5 are saved separately and are explicitly excluded from shared UI output cards. However, browser localStorage is unencrypted. If sharing a device, clear data after your session.
