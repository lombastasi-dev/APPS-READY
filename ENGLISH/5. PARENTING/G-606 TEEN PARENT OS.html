Privacy, Security & Safety Guidelines

How Teen Parent OS™ handles your information and maintains local privacy.

1. Local Storage Privacy
All inputs, snapshot updates, and generated scripts remain strictly inside your browser's local storage (localStorage). No server transmission, accounts, remote databases, or cloud synchronization are used.
2. Scope & Safety Boundaries
Teen Parent OS™ is an educational, reflective communication toolkit. It is not a clinical assessment tool, diagnostic instrument, or therapy substitute. If you suspect immediate physical danger, severe distress, or emergency safety risks, consult qualified local professional services immediately.
3. Data Management
You can clear your stored data at any time from the "My History" tab or by clearing your browser's site data. Avoid entering sensitive personally identifiable information (such as full legal names or addresses) into free-text fields.
Teen Parent OS™ is designed for practical parenting communication support only. Not medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or legal advice.
