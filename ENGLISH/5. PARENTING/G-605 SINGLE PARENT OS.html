Privacy & Local Data Control

Your household data remains entirely in your browser.

Zero Cloud Transmission

SingleParentOS™ operates completely inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. No accounts, remote databases, AI servers, or external tracking tools are used. Your financial figures, schedule inputs, co-parent scripts, and reflection entries never leave this device.

Browser Storage Management

Data is stored locally under versioned localStorage namespaces (singleparentos_v2_state). Clearing browser cache or private storage will reset saved entries.
